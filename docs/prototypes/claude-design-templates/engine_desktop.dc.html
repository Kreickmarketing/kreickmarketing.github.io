T.O.E. (Training Observability Engine) collects anonymized, role-based data

Every Kinney AI deployment—whether in Microsoft Copilot, Salesforce, Workday, Slack, or any enterprise environment—includes the Training Observability Engine, which monitors learning activity, tracks adoption, and provides real-time visibility into AI capability building. All data is distilled into simple, actionable dashboards that guide the next wave of transformation.
