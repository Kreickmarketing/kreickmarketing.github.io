km-steps · desktop 1280

How I made it

  1. One

    Set the style

    I chose the look in Midjourney, then had an LLM break my references into a reusable style prompt: warm stage light, red-orange palette, 85mm lens at f/1.8, Kodak Portra 400 grain.

  2. Two

    Cast the singers

    I swapped in the faces I wanted, keeping each shot's composition, microphone and pose, then directed new angles and emotion from the same performer.

  3. Three

    Build the first and last frames

    I applied the saved style to every shot with faces and wardrobe locked, then made a wide first frame and a close last frame for the video to travel between.

  4. Four

    Sync the performance

    I fed my vocal track into WAN 2.7 Video to lip-sync each singer to the lyrics, then cut the edit and the kinetic type in Adobe CC.
