Full-width dark-navy site footer with wordmark, tagline, contact block, and a hairline-divided legal row.

```jsx
<Footer
  brandName="Clearmark"
  tagline="Built for real people, in real jobs, right now."
  email="hello@clearmark.bz"
  address="Based in Austin, TX - Working Globally"
  links={["Privacy Policy", "Terms & Conditions"]}
/>
```

Stacks to a single column on mobile (see `assets/footer-mobile-ref.png`).
